Dribbling Drills

High-level technical routines to improve control and rhythm.

TRAINING FUNDAMENTALS

  • Eyes Up Stay focused on the court, never the ball.
  • Pound the Ball Dribble with maximum force to build strength.
  • Intensity Maintain 100% speed for 60 seconds per drill.
  • Stance Keep your base low and knees bent.
Warmup

Low Fingertip Dribble

Quick touches using only your fingertips below knee height.

Essential

Crossover Control

Sharp, wide crosses keeping the ball below waist level.

Advanced

Figure Eight

Fluid motion through the legs without stopping the ball.

Technique

Behind the Back

Tight wraps around the waist while maintaining forward speed.

Pro

In-and-Out Hesi

Ball manipulation to freeze defenders and create space.

Elite

Combo Burner

Stringing multiple moves together at high intensity.
